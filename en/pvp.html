PvP & teams

⚔ Combat tag

Hit a player or get hit by one and you are in combat for 20 s (a red bar). Logging out in combat = death: your items drop and whoever hit you last gets the kill. No elytra while in combat.

Clash of legends

Kill a player who carries a legend with a legend in your hand and your weapon gets +0.5 ★ (up to 4 ★ – the last stars only come from the End).

Maces and Mending

Vanilla maces can't be crafted on this server. After a smash (a hit while falling) every mace has a 10 s cooldown – another smash in that time only deals a normal hit.

Villagers don't sell Mending – you only find it in structure chests and by fishing.

Protected spawn and Ancient Forges

Spawn and the area around every Ancient Forge can be mined and blown up, but nothing drops and after 25 minutes everything grows back. Placed blocks disappear. It never hurts anyone – a block never comes back inside a player or vanishes from under one.

Teams

Teams come from the Forgebound Teams plugin. /team opens a menu where everything can be clicked: create a team (up to 3 players), invites, a tag and color in front of the names, team chat (!message), allies (@message), wars and the team leaderboard. Teammates and allies can't hurt each other – not even with legend abilities.

Forgebound is a plugin for Paper 1.21.11. This wiki is generated from the config, so the numbers match the server.
